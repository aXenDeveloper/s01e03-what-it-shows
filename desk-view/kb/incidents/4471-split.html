How each one is told apart

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so day and month swap. It comes in through every channel. “Booked for 2 July. The invite says 7 February.”

hint: tenants on booking-v3 show MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. People type what the hint says, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. It only comes in through the booking form, and only from booking-v3 tenants. The swap looks the same as parser, so subject and body cannot tell them apart: the tenant’s form and the channel can. Fixing the parser does not fix the hint.

one-day: the date moves by one day, not by a swap. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” “Every booking in June is one day off. Only June.” The desk already read these reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but filed them under parser by tenant. A one-day move between time zones is a different bug from a day/month swap, so it gets its own ticket.

Why one ticket hid them

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject alone. “Booking on the wrong date” is the same subject for a parser report and a hint report.
  • Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant. The tag is a guess, and it disagrees with the problem often (see the table).
  • The export repeats reports where its pages overlap. Count unique ids only.

What the desk does now

  1. New date reports: place them with #lib/kb/problems (tenant, form, channel), not by subject.
  2. Merge into the problem’s own ticket, not into #4471.
  3. A report that reads as a one-day move goes to one-day, whatever its tenant.
